Delete your Banigoba account

This page explains how to delete your account in the Banigoba app, published by Colours Inc., and what happens to your data when you do.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Banigoba and sign in.
  2. Open the menu and tap Settings.
  3. Tap Privacy & safety, then Delete account.
  4. Confirm. Your account is deleted straight away and you are signed out.

Can't open the app?

Email support@banigoba.app from the email address on your account, with the subject “Delete my Banigoba account” and your @username. We will confirm the request is yours and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, email, @username and profile photo.
  • Your wardrobe, clothing photos and collections.
  • Your looks, makeovers, try-ons and the photos used for them, including your body profile.
  • Your comments, affirmations, messages, follows and saved items.
  • Your orders and delivery addresses.
  • Your notification token and settings.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Encrypted backups may still hold your data for up to 30 days, after which they are overwritten.
  • Nothing else is kept. We do not keep a copy of your account after it is deleted.

Deleting your account cannot be undone.

Delete some data without closing your account

You can delete individual looks, clothes, comments and messages in the app at any time. To ask us to delete anything else, email support@banigoba.app.
