Privacy Policy

Banigoba is a virtual wardrobe, styling and fashion community app operated by Colours Inc. ("we", "us"). This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

What we collect

Account details
Your name, email address, @username and profile photo. If you sign in with Apple or Google we receive the name and email those services share with us (Apple may give us a private relay address).
What you add
Photos of your clothes and of yourself, looks, makeovers, comments, affirmations, messages, saved items and style preferences. Your body profile (photos, sizes and measurements) is private to you.
Location
With your permission, your approximate location while you use the app, to show weather-based outfit ideas and people and shops near you. We store only your city and a coarse area, never a precise trail.
Orders
Items you order and the delivery or collection address you give, shared with the shop or provider who fills the order. Payment is made on delivery or collection; we do not collect card details.
Device data
A push notification token, so we can send the notifications you have turned on, and basic diagnostic information when something goes wrong.

How we use it

  • To run the app: your wardrobe, outfit ideas, makeovers, try-ons, the community feeds and orders.
  • To keep people safe: reviewing reports, removing content that breaks our Terms, and enforcing blocks.
  • To send notifications you have chosen to receive.

We do not sell your personal information, show third-party advertising, or track you across other companies' apps and websites.

AI features and your photos

Only after you allow it in the app, the photos you choose for an AI feature are sent to:

  • Google Gemini (Google LLC) to tag clothes and to create makeovers and try-ons;
  • our background-removal service to cut a garment out of its photo.

They are used only to produce the result you asked for. We do not use your photos to train AI models. You can withdraw permission at any time in Settings › Privacy & safety; AI features then ask again before sending anything.

Who else processes your data

  • Supabase: our database, sign-in and file storage.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and Apple Push Notification service: notifications.
  • Sign in with Apple and Google Sign-In: if you choose them.
  • WeatherAPI.com: receives your approximate location to return the local weather.
  • Vercel: hosts the Banigoba website.

Each processes data only on our instructions and under its own security commitments.

Who can see what you post

Each look is private until you share it, and you choose who sees it: friends, followers or everyone. Your email, address and body profile are never shown to other people.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account is open. You can delete your account at any time in Settings › Privacy & safety › Delete account. This permanently removes your profile, looks, wardrobe, photos, comments, messages and order history from our systems. Backups are overwritten within 30 days. See how to delete your account, including without the app.

Your rights

You can see and change most of your information in the app. To ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or anything else about your privacy, contact us below. Depending on where you live you may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Banigoba is not for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, and access to it is limited by row-level security so people see only what they are allowed to.

Changes

We will post any change here and tell you in the app if it is significant.
