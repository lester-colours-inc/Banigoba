# Banigoba 1.1.0 (build 2) — tester release, Friday 23 October 2026

Everything from the 14 October app review that is built so far. The full
checklist is in [docs/review-2026-10-14](../review-2026-10-14/README.md).

## What's new for testers

**Home**
- A large "Create My Fashion Makeover" button. It first asks: **Hairstyle** or
  **Outfit & more**.
- My Fashion Makeover shows your looks and your friends' and community looks.
  Community shows other people's looks and upcoming events, with "Add event".
- Tap anywhere on a post to open it. The wardrobe icon fills when a look is
  saved, and a sheet lets you file it under a category.
- **Post** (⋮ menu) decides who sees a look; **Send to** (share icon) sends it
  to a salon, shop, designer, WhatsApp or another app.
- Repost and Quote are always there; a private look asks to be shared first.
- Only you see how many affirmations your look got.

**Makeover**
- "My New Look": your photo, then **Describe it** or **Use a look you love**,
  then what should change (you must pick one).
- "Find a look you love": from people you follow, Fashion Twins, saved looks
  (by category), looks you affirmed, or the community, with search.

**You and your people**
- Everyone chooses a unique **@username** once. Search people by name or
  @username. Emails are private.
- Change your profile photo by tapping it. Follower numbers show only to you.
- New menu pages: **My Fashion Twins**, **My Fashion Idols** ("Style me like
  …"), **My Hair Stylists** (your salons, hairstyles you like, "Try this on
  me").
- **My Circle**: your own salons, shops and designers come first in Send to.

**Smart Wardrobe**
- My Clothes, Saved Looks (by category), Shops & Designers.
- Add item asks for size, shoulders and length.
- "I wore this today", and "Not worn in 10 months" reminders.
- My Daily Dress Up: share today's dress-up; see friends' dress-ups.

# Release-day runbook (Friday 23 October)

Order matters: the new database rules refuse the **old** app's feed, so the
new build goes out first.

1. **Ship the build.** Merge to `main` (CI builds the APK), or build locally:
   ```
   flutter build apk --release --dart-define-from-file=dart_defines.json
   ```
   Send `build/app/outputs/flutter-apk/app-release.apk` to testers.
2. **Check the rules once more** (local emulator, needs Java 21+):
   ```
   JAVA_HOME=/opt/homebrew/opt/openjdk/libexec/openjdk.jdk/Contents/Home npm --prefix test_rules test
   ```
3. **Deploy the rules** (I-2 private posts, I-4 no self-made admins, I-5
   curator verification, U-5 private emails):
   ```
   firebase deploy --only firestore:rules --project appdemo-b9e3e
   ```
4. **Check on a phone with the new build:** Home, Community and My Daily Dress
   Up load; a friends-only look shows to a friend and not to a stranger;
   username search works; a curator can verify a shop.
5. **Move remaining emails off public profiles** (needs a service-account
   key in `GOOGLE_APPLICATION_CREDENTIALS`):
   ```
   cd functions/scripts
   node migrate_private_emails.js --dry-run
   node migrate_private_emails.js
   ```

## If something goes wrong

- **Feeds fail after the rules deploy:** put back the previous rules (commit
  `dd3a961`) and deploy them:
  ```
  git show dd3a961:firestore.rules > /tmp/firestore.rules.previous
  cp firestore.rules /tmp/firestore.rules.new
  cp /tmp/firestore.rules.previous firestore.rules
  firebase deploy --only firestore:rules --project appdemo-b9e3e
  cp /tmp/firestore.rules.new firestore.rules
  ```
  Note this reopens I-2, I-4 and I-5 until fixed and redeployed.
- **A tester still has the old app:** their Home feed shows "Posts could not
  be loaded" until they install 1.1.0.
